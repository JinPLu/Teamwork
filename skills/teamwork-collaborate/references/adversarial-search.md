# Adversarial Challenge

Use this method only when the user explicitly requests an adversarial challenge,
independent stress test, or strict search closure. Root keeps ownership of the
dialogue and recommendation; Challenger supplies read-only critique and never
decides for the user.

Freeze the decision, criteria, protected boundaries, direct evidence, leading
option, strongest credible alternative, and the uncertainty the challenge must
reduce. Give a Challenger only its bounded target. A claimed independent view
needs a genuinely distinct task context; Root cannot simulate it when the role
or isolation is unavailable.

Ask the Challenger to seek disconfirming evidence, expose the strongest hidden
assumption, test the strongest alternative, and state what evidence would
change its conclusion. Dispatch several challenges only when their targets are
independent and bounded. There is no prescribed number of agents, hypotheses,
trials, or passes.

Root integrates the critique with the live comparison and reports what
survived, what changed, unresolved evidence, independence limits, and the
strongest downside. Do not call a bounded challenge exhaustive, silently widen
its scope, or turn its conclusion into the user's decision.
