# Deep Research

Every Research activation uses this full method. Freeze the decision question,
intended use, scope, freshness cutoff, public-source and privacy boundaries,
material claims, search axes, and what would make the evidence sufficient.

Build a source census across the source classes relevant to the brief and a
claim-to-evidence map or ledger. Iterate by the most consequential evidence
gap: retrieve, read the strongest primary material, update claim coverage,
identify contradiction or uncertainty, and search the next discriminator.
Supplied sources are seeds, not automatic proof or a search boundary.

For every material claim, retain direct support, counterevidence, inference,
confidence, date or version, and citation. Record why rejected sources are
unsuitable and what was not found. Split work only across independent evidence
areas and keep one synthesis owner.

Audit whether the material claims are covered; source volume alone is not
coverage. Return the supported conclusion, contradictions, residual
uncertainty, coverage result, and the explicit stop basis. Stop when the scope
is answered or further retrieval is unlikely to change the conclusion, not
when an arbitrary source or search count is reached.
